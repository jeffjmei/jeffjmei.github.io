## Writing style (project pages, _projects/*.md)

- Bold topic sentences are punchlines, not labels — reading only the bold text should tell the full story.
- Keep bold clauses short (under ~10 words) and asserted, not explained. Explanation goes in the sentence after.
- No throat-clearing, rhetorical questions, or "much effort is spent on X" filler.
- Cut anything not earning its place (decorative equations, intro paragraphs, sections) rather than padding.
- Prefer concrete mechanism ("residuals end up correlated with X") over abstraction ("this introduces bias").
- When drafting, offer 2-3 short alternatives for a line rather than one long paragraph.
